# Surface: /concept-b — Premium Minimal

Mode: persuade. Action: upload a bill.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One object, one sentence, one action. The bill itself is the product shot. Refuses dense marketing sections and cards.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite black (#0A0B0A) chapters alternating with porcelain white (#F5F6F3); electric green used only as light (a scan line, a focus ring, the primary action). Bodoni Moda numerals set like a type specimen; Hanken Grotesk for everything read.
STORY: A quiet statement, the bill turns in space and is read, evidence lands as typography, five services read as chapters, real people speak, then the action returns.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred statement "L'estalvi és ara." at display scale, bill object below rising into view, translucent nav with the action button always present.
FORM: pinned by brief (B. Premium Minimal, Apple-inspired). seed: none (pinned). Raise from declined challenger "Emigre bitmap specimen": one form repeated at many sizes; the numeral is the hero of the evidence section.
RISK: minimal can read empty; mitigated by scroll-scrubbed object choreography.
