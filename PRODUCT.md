## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, specified by the user. Animation base: GSAP + ScrollTrigger (user requirement); Lenis, Motion and Three.js allowed when justified.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):
- **Households (particulars)** in Catalonia who pay electricity, gas, fibre/mobile, alarm and insurance bills and suspect they pay too much, but have no time to compare offers.
- **Businesses (empreses)**: small and medium companies with one or several supply points who want someone to review contracts, contracted power and renewals for them.

Their job: hand over a bill once and get a clear, trustworthy answer on whether they can pay less, without changing habits or dealing with companies themselves.

## Product Purpose

Assessoria 3.0 is an independent advisory for electricity, gas, telecommunications, alarms and insurance. It studies the client's bills, compares offers across the retailers it works with, manages the switch, and reviews the bills again every year. The service is free for the client (the advisory is paid a commission by the companies; the price for the client is the same or better than going direct).

The new website's commercial protagonist is **SavEnergy IA**: a comparator where the visitor uploads an electricity or gas bill and receives a savings estimate. Success = visitors upload a bill or request a free study.

## Positioning

A human advisory with local offices across the four Catalan provinces that pairs its people with an AI bill reader (SavEnergy IA). Not a price-comparison website: it reads the real bill, proposes, switches and keeps reviewing yearly.

## Product Principles

1. The bill is the entry point: every path leads to uploading a bill or requesting a free study.
2. Prove with the mechanism, not with adjectives: show how the bill is read and compared.
3. Honesty over hype: simulated numbers are always labelled; real evidence is quoted exactly.
4. People behind the AI: offices, phones and names stay visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Broad audience including older households: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard use, reduced-motion respected, body text ≥16px.
